## Meta URLs after deployment

If the site is deployed at `https://YOUR-DOMAIN.example`:

- Privacy Policy: `https://YOUR-DOMAIN.example/privacy-policy.html`
- Terms of Service: `https://YOUR-DOMAIN.example/terms-of-service.html`
- Data deletion: `https://YOUR-DOMAIN.example/data-deletion.html`

For Meta, use the Privacy Policy URL in the App settings. Use the Data Deletion URL for the data deletion instructions field.

## Important

These are practical template legal pages for an app using Instagram APIs. Review them for your actual data storage, hosting providers, business/legal entity, retention practices, and jurisdiction before publishing them as your final legal documents.
