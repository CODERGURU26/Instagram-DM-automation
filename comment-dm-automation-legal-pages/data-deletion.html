Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of information associated with your use of Comment DM Automation.

1. Disconnect the App from Instagram

You can first remove the App from your Instagram connected apps / apps and websites settings. Disconnecting the App stops the authorization connection from being used for future API access, subject to any data that must be retained for legal or security purposes.

2. Request deletion

Email: guruuu2468@gmail.com

Subject: Comment DM Automation - Data Deletion Request

In your request, include:

  • The Instagram username associated with the request.
  • The Instagram account ID, if available.
  • The email address you want us to use for the deletion request.
  • A brief statement confirming that you want your data deleted.

3. What we will do

After receiving a valid request, we will verify the request as reasonably necessary and delete or anonymize data associated with the request that we are not required to retain. This may include stored webhook payloads, comment or message records, account connection information, and related application records.

4. Exceptions

We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or establish or defend legal claims. Any retained information will be handled according to our Privacy Policy.

5. Processing time

We aim to process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period after verification. If additional information is needed to verify the request, we may contact you using the information provided.

6. Contact

For questions about deletion, contact guruuu2468@gmail.com.
