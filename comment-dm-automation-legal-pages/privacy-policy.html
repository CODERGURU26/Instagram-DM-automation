Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Comment DM Automation ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you connect an Instagram professional account to the App.

1. What the App does

Comment DM Automation is an automation service designed to receive eligible Instagram webhook events, process Instagram comments and messages according to configured automation rules, and send permitted Instagram private replies or messages through Meta's APIs.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you enable and the permissions you grant, the App may process:

  • Instagram professional account identifiers and basic account information made available through the authorized Instagram API.
  • Instagram comment information, including comment text, comment identifiers, media identifiers, timestamps, and information needed to identify the commenter or the relevant conversation.
  • Instagram messaging information required to receive, respond to, or manage permitted conversations.
  • Webhook event payloads sent to the App by Meta.
  • Technical information such as request timestamps, error information, and server logs used to operate and secure the service.

3. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide Instagram comment and messaging automation.
  • Receive and process webhook events.
  • Send automated replies or messages that you configure or authorize.
  • Authenticate and maintain the connection between the App and your Instagram account.
  • Monitor, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the App.
  • Comply with applicable law and valid legal requests.

4. Meta and Instagram data

The App uses Meta's Instagram APIs and processes data made available through the permissions you authorize. Your use of Instagram remains subject to Instagram's and Meta's applicable terms and policies.

We do not sell Instagram information or use Instagram data for unrelated advertising purposes.

5. Access tokens and security

Access tokens are used to authenticate permitted API requests. We take reasonable technical measures to protect credentials and limit access to authorized systems. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should also protect your Meta and Instagram credentials.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Where information is no longer required, we aim to delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure providers that help us operate the App, subject to appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate abuse or security incidents.

8. Your choices and rights

You may disconnect the App from your Instagram account through Instagram's connected apps settings. You may also request deletion of data associated with your use of the App by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use Instagram and applicable law. We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect information from children for the purpose of providing this service.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our data practices, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date above indicates when this version became effective.
